# Privacy Policy for Spin My Wheel

**Last updated:** February 3, 2026

## Overview

Spin My Wheel is a simple wheel spinner application that helps you make random
selections. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

Spin My Wheel is designed to respect your privacy:

- **No Data Collection**: The app does not collect any personal data, usage
  statistics, or analytics.
- **No Internet Connection Required**: The app works completely offline and does
  not connect to any servers.
- **No Third-Party Services**: We do not use any third-party analytics,
  advertising, or tracking services.
- **Local Storage Only**: All data you enter (names, wheel entries) is stored
  locally on your device only.

## Data Storage

Any data you enter into the app (such as names or entries for the wheel) is:

- Stored locally on your device only
- Never transmitted to any external servers
- Under your complete control
- Can be deleted at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app

## Permissions

The app does not require any special permissions beyond basic app functionality.

## Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any information from anyone, including children under
13, the app is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted
in this document with an updated "Last updated" date.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data:

- There is no data to access, modify, or delete from our servers
